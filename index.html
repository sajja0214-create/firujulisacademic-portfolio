01 / RESEARCH PROFILE

I am an agriculture graduate from the University of Rajshahi, Bangladesh. My research interests centre on how pollination, floral biology and crop management influence fruit production. I have worked on jackfruit assisted pollination, litchi pollination and papaya floral biology.

01

FRUIT CROP · POLLINATION

Assisted pollination and fruit set in jackfruit

Investigated natural pollination, assisted hand pollination and pollinator exclusion across four locations in Bangladesh over two flowering seasons. The study recorded 433 flower/inflorescence treatment observations and compared fruit-set responses under contrasting agro-ecological conditions.
